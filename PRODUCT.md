# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Public visitors (no account): browse a small tech store's catalog, search/filter, open a product, and send an enquiry about stock or price.
- Store administrators: the only accounts; manage products, categories, enquiries and business info from the private panel.

## Product Purpose

TechStore is a fictional technology retailer built as a university assignment (TPO Aplicaciones Interactivas, 2.º cuatrimestre 2026). The public site presents the shop and its catalog; success is a visitor finding a product (or not) and sending an enquiry. There is no cart or checkout: purchase intent goes through the contact form.

## Positioning

A neighbourhood tech shop (consoles, PCs, phones, games, accessories) with prices in ARS and live stock, reachable without registering.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19 + React Router 7 + Vite, hand-written CSS (no UI framework). Node and npm only.
- Layered architecture must be preserved (pages → hooks/services → API).
- Data comes from the REST API: products (name, category, description, images, price, availableQuantity, isFeatured, createdAt), categories, business info (name, description, address, phone, socialNetworks, openingHours).
- Spanish (es-AR) copy throughout.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: TechStore.
- All existing Spanish copy on the public site is preserved verbatim through redesigns (confirmed 2026-10-19).

## Evidence on Hand

- Product images are uploaded by admins; the seed provides demo products. No testimonials, reviews, press, or customer counts exist — do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Finding a product beats being impressed by the page.
2. Price and stock are the facts visitors came for; show them plainly.
3. No account wall: the enquiry is the conversion.
